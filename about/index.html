About me

From microelectronics to computer systems, following an interest in low-level mechanisms into GPU observability and AI inference engineering.

I am DQR-Happy, an M.Sc. student in Computer Science and Technology at Guilin University of Electronic Technology. My undergraduate background at Hunan University is in microelectronics and integrated circuits.

My work centers on GPU tracing and performance observation, alongside engineering practice in LLM inference systems. I am interested in questions at the hardware–software boundary: how to observe execution reliably, how collection costs affect complete workflows, and how kernel or runtime changes translate into real-task benefits.

I aim to keep research methods close to implementation: define the problem, understand the mechanism, build the system, and use reproducible experiments to see what it actually solves.
